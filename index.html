About CL'S Brownies

Baking from scratch, one batch at a time.

CL’S Brownies is a student-run homemade bakery in Dublin, born from an obsession with getting brownies right. We believe brownies should be dense, rich, and intensely chocolatey — never dry, cakey, or rushed.

Because every order is baked by hand in small quantities, each box receives genuine care. Whether you are ordering a personal treat, celebrating a birthday, or fueling a study session, we bake each batch fresh for you.
